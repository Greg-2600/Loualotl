# Louxolotl

A cute, browser-based axolotl game. Swim around a Mexican canal, hunt for snacks, dodge the nasty fish, and soak up a ton of fun facts about axolotls!

Play it by opening `axolotl_game.html` in any modern web browser — no installation, no build step, no assets needed.

## Features

- **Friendly, hungry axolotl** — You play as a little axolotl in the crystal-blue waters of Lake Xochimilco.
- **Two kinds of fish** — Toothless fish are harmless and make a tasty snack. Fish with teeth will bite back, so keep your distance!
- **Chomp everything edible** — Eat worms, shrimp, insects, and the toothless fish. The other axolotls will join in on the hunt too.
- **Oxygen matters** — Your oxygen slowly depletes over time. Swim to the surface (air zone at the top) to gulp air and refill. The lower your oxygen gets, the slower you'll swim, so you'll need to plan your dives.
- **Damage & regeneration** — Getting bitten by a toothed fish costs a limb. Collect the glowing green sparkles to heal back faster!
- **Loads of axolotl facts** — After every 10 bites, and every 2 minutes while playing, you'll get a new axolotl fact. There are 50 to discover!
- **Cute details** — Feathered gills that flutter with your swim speed, a wiggly tail, happy twirls, bubble puffs, and little underwater ambiance.
- **Easy controls** — Mouse, touch, arrow keys, or WASD all work. Press `1` to zip straight to the surface, and `2` to dive to the lake bed.

## How to Play

1. Open `axolotl_game.html` in your web browser.
2. Move around using your mouse, touch, `←↑→↓`, or `WASD`.
3. Eat worms, shrimp, insects, and **toothless fish**. They'll boost your food count and might teach you something new.
4. **Dodge fish with teeth** — if they touch you while you're vulnerable, you'll lose a limb and start regenerating.
5. Keep an eye on your **Oxygen** bar. Swim to the very top of the screen to refill it. At low oxygen you'll swim slower, so head up in plenty of time!
6. While regenerating, chase the glowing green sparkles — they'll speed up your healing.
7. Learn cool axolotl facts along the way. Press `1` for a quick trip up for air, or `2` to dive back down.

## Tips

- Toothless fish are your best "free" food — they're slower than the toothed ones, so they're easy to catch.
- Press `1` anytime you want to ride straight up to the surface — perfect if you spot danger or notice your oxygen getting low.
- The AI axolotls also eat toothless fish. If one beats you to a snack, don't worry — more will spawn!
- Axolotls are neotenic: they stay in their "larval" form their whole lives, keeping those feathery gills. How cool is that?

## Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Mouse` / `Touch` | Swim to wherever you tap/click |
| `Arrow Keys` / `WASD` | Steer in the usual directions |
| `1` | Zip to the surface and stay until full oxygen |
| `2` | Dive to the lake bed |
| `M` | Toggle sound |
| `N` | Toggle underwater ambience |
